Don't just sit there...

Back pain, stiff legs, sore neck, shoulders ache, sciatica, weak glutes (whatever they are), slowed metabolism, poor circulation, low energy, low mood, the list goes on. Sitting down at your desk all day will create problems. You need to stand up every now and then. But the work is too engaging and you forget.

What you need is a small reminder. Some type of pesting message telling to stand up. That's what this web site is here to do. Set how long you want to sit down before you receive a notification, telling you to stand up. You can also set how long it waits before it tells you to sit back down. Stand up, sit down, repeat, it's that simple.

There are different options available to help you customize how it works. One way of doing things is to switch between sitting and standing. You can also use fixed times of the day to trigger the stand up events.

You will need to have the web page open for it to work. You don't need to be viewing it, just let it exist somewhere in the background.
